# 02 — CAMPAIGN STRATEGY
NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — Mauritius Chapter | Oct 2 – Nov 15, 2026

---

## 1. GOALS & KPIs

**Primary goal:** 100 registered participants for the Mauritius chapter.
**Secondary:** follower growth, reach, meaningful engagement, registration conversion, understanding of Space Apps (what/how/challenges/teams), non-programmer participation, stakeholder visibility, reusable post-event content.

Metrics hierarchy (full targets in `09-KPI-Tracker.csv`):
- Awareness → reach, video views, story views
- Interest → profile visits, saves, engagement rate
- Conversion → link clicks, registrations, FB event responses
- Community → comments, shares, UGC, team-formation interactions
- Event → live story views, interactions, check-ins
- Post-event → shares, follows, retention

**Rule:** follower count is a hygiene metric, never the north star. Registrations + engagement quality lead.

## 3. AUDIENCE MATRIX (what they care about / blockers / winning content / CTA)

### A. Students (university/tertiary)
- **Care about:** experience for CV, meeting people, learning something real, free events.
- **Blockers:** "too hard", exam pressure, no team, never heard of it.
- **Winning content:** peer stories, "no experience needed" proof, fun team content, what's in it for CV/skills.
- **CTA:** "Register + bring a friend" / "Comment your course to find teammates."

### B. Developers / programmers
- **Care about:** real data, interesting tech problems, portfolio projects.
- **Blockers:** "it's just for space nerds", time, unclear value.
- **Winning content:** NASA open data + APIs, technical challenge walkthroughs (SAR, SPHEREx, AI dashboards), example project directions.
- **CTA:** "Pick a technical challenge and register" / "Save the challenge map."

### C. Designers & creatives
- **Care about:** meaningful creative work, portfolio, recognition.
- **Blockers:** "hackathons are for coders."
- **Winning content:** storytelling/art challenges (Abandoned but Not Forgotten, Earth Information Jukebox, game design), proof that design wins hackathons.
- **CTA:** "Designers, this is your challenge" / tag a designer friend.

### D. Scientists / STEM people
- **Care about:** real science, NASA data, credibility, impact.
- **Winning content:** Earth science challenges (Trend Detective, SARs, Field Shift, Hot Spots, analogs), researcher-friendly tone.
- **Blockers:** "hackathon = coding marathon."
- **CTA:** "Bring your science brain — coders are optional" / share with lab group.

### E. Non-technical problem solvers
- **Care about:** ideas, impact, organizing, communication.
- **Blockers:** "I can't contribute without code."
- **Winning content:** role explainers (PM, research, pitch, storytelling), team role examples.
- **CTA:** "Your idea + their code = a team" / comment your skill.

### F. Space enthusiasts
- **Care about:** NASA, astronomy, Moon/Mars, community.
- **Blockers:** none strong — high-intent audience.
- **Winning content:** challenge teasers (lunar browser, Martian map, SPHEREx), NASA imagery, trivia.
- **CTA:** share with fellow enthusiasts + register.

### G. Educators / researchers
- **Care about:** student development, curriculum links, credible programs.
- **Blockers:** time, need institutional angle.
- **Winning content:** LinkedIn professional posts, youth challenges (mission trainer, design game), school participation info.
- **CTA:** "Bring this to your students/class" + contact us [CONTACT EMAIL].

### H. Industry professionals
- **Care about:** innovation, networking, talent, CSR.
- **Blockers:** "it's a student thing."
- **Winning content:** LinkedIn professional narrative, mentor/judge roles, company team angle.
- **CTA:** mentor, judge, or sponsor → message us.

### I. Organizations & institutions
- **Care about:** visibility, ecosystem credibility, talent pipeline.
- **Blockers:** not knowing the chapter, unclear ask.
- **Winning content:** partnership announcements, thank-you posts, co-branded content.
- **CTA:** repost + partner with us (see `06-Stakeholder-Amplification.csv`).

## 5. PLATFORM STRATEGY (summary — full sheet in `05-Platform-Plan.csv`)

**Instagram — the volume + discovery engine.** Feed 3–4/wk (carousels + reels), Stories 4–5 sets/wk, Highlights for permanent education. Reels made phone-first: face-to-camera, NASA public-domain B-roll, template graphics — no expensive production. Stories carry polls/quizzes/countdowns; feed carries the evergreen value.

**Facebook — reach beyond Instagram.** Same core content, longer captions, native FB Event page [FB EVENT LINK], community groups, parents/professionals/organizations. Announcements + FAQs live here in text form.

**LinkedIn — credibility for stakeholders.** 2/wk, never an IG repost. Organizer POV posts, university/company framing, mentor/judge/partner content, professional project stories. Posts signed by real people outperform page-only posts.

**Cross-posting rule:** same visual asset allowed; caption rewritten per platform (IG short+emotive, FB conversational+link, LinkedIn professional+context).

## 7. CREATIVE DIRECTION — "THE NEXT FRONTIER"

- **Feel:** futuristic, intelligent, energetic, youthful, locally relevant, credible.
- **Palette:** deep space navy #0B1026 · electric cyan #4FD1FF · solar orange #FF7A45 · star white.
- **Devices:** mission-control grid lines, starfield textures, "transmission" frames, consistent corner-locked series badges.
- **Typography:** one display font for series titles, one clean sans for body.
- **NASA imagery:** use NASA public-domain photos/video per NASA Media Usage Guidelines. Never alter NASA insignia; never imply NASA endorsement of the local chapter. Official NASA branding stays on official assets.
- **Recurring visual:** every Challenge File opens with the same "CHALLENGE FILE 0X" badge + theme lockup → instant recognizability.

## 9. CONTENT STRATEGY RULES (applied in calendar)

1. Not one long advertisement — education:excitement:utility ratio ≈ 50:30:20.
2. Mix education, excitement, practical info, people, storytelling, community, urgency.
3. One primary objective per post (column 6 in calendar).
4. Max ~40 words on any graphic; details live in captions.
5. Technical concepts simplified without dumbing down (e.g., "SAR = radar that sees through clouds").
6. Never assume NASA Space Apps jargon; every series starts from zero.
7. ~8 direct registration posts max; all other posts carry softer CTAs.
8. Real people on camera wherever possible (volunteers, organizers, participants).
9. Shareable: challenge files, quizzes, "tag a friend" moments.
10. LinkedIn-safe: professional, factual, no hype.
11. No fabricated statistics — only official cumulative program numbers (cited).
12. No fake urgency — registration is officially open through Nov 15; urgency = real milestones + venue capacity [VERIFY].
13. No superlatives ("biggest/first/best") unless verified.
14. Speculative solution ideas are labeled "possible directions (our suggestion)".
15. OFFICIAL vs LOCAL vs RECOMMENDATION always labeled.
