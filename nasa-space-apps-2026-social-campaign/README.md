2. Choose "Replace spreadsheet" and set **Separator type: Comma** (values are quote-protected).
3. In Excel: open the `.csv` directly, or Data → From Text/CSV (delimiter: comma).

## 7. What I would NOT do (10 common mistakes to avoid)

1. **Publish 14 text-heavy challenge screenshots in a row.** Use the Challenge Files system instead.
2. **Copy-paste identical captions to IG, FB and LinkedIn.** Each platform gets its own copy.
3. **Claim "biggest / first / best hackathon in Mauritius"** — unverified; breaks credibility.
4. **Use fake urgency.** Registration is officially open through Nov 15. Urgency comes from real milestones (venue capacity [VERIFY], team formation, statements release).
5. **Invent venue, sponsors, partners, handles, or statistics.** Use placeholders until verified.
6. **Use NASA insignia (meatball/worm) as if NASA endorses the local chapter**, or alter official logos. Use NASA public-domain imagery per NASA Media Usage Guidelines without implying endorsement.
7. **Turn every post into "REGISTER NOW."** Max ~8 direct registration posts; everything else educates.
8. **Ignore non-coders after week 1.** Designers, storytellers, scientists and organizers are the differentiators of a strong local chapter.
9. **Spend event days editing Reels** while moments pass. Capture first, edit later (batch system in `14-Workflow-Team-Risks-PostEvent.md`).
10. **Go silent after Nov 15.** The post-event window (recaps, project spotlights, testimonials) is what builds a permanent community and next year's audience.
