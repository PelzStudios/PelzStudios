# 14 — PRODUCTION WORKFLOW, TEAM ROLES, RISKS, POST-EVENT SEQUENCE

## 1. WEEKLY PRODUCTION LOOP (volunteer-friendly)

**Sunday (30 min):** review last week's numbers (reach, saves, comments) → pick content for next week from calendar → confirm any official milestones.
**Monday (1–2 h):** write all captions for the week (IG/FB/LinkedIn versions) in one sitting.
**Tuesday (1–2 h):** batch-design all static/carousel graphics from templates; batch-record 1–2 Reels in one location session.
**Wednesday:** schedule everything in Meta Business Suite (IG+FB) and LinkedIn scheduler.
**Thursday–Saturday:** post stories live (10 min/day), respond to comments/DMs within 24 h, monitor challenges quizzes.
**Event weekend:** see live-coverage system below.

## 2. TEAM ROLES

**Full version (4 people):**
| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Social Media Lead | Strategy, calendar ownership, captions, scheduling, analytics, approvals |
| Content Creator | Reels (script/record/edit), on-camera personality |
| Designer | Templates, carousels, challenge files, highlight covers |
| Photographer/Videographer | Event coverage, shot list execution, file transfer |

**Lean version (2 people + borrowed help):**
- Person A = Social Media Lead + Content Creator (strategy, captions, reels, stories).
- Person B = Designer + Photographer (templates + event shooting).
- Borrow 1 volunteer on Nov 14–15 purely for photo/video so Person A can post.

**Event-day minimum:** 1 shooter + 1 poster. If only one person exists: shoot in 2-hour batches, then upload in 15-minute bursts — never edit during the event.

## 3. LIVE COVERAGE SYSTEM (Nov 14–15)

1. **Capture in batches:** shooter works on a 2-hour cycle (07:30, 10:00, 13:00, 16:00, 18:00) covering the shot list, then hands off files.
2. **Schedule beforehand:** Day 1/Day 2 feed posts, submission reminder, thank-you draft, story templates, poll graphics — all pre-built.
3. **Live-only items:** real moments (opening, submissions, group photo), participant reactions, polls on real events.
4. **Rule:** no editing during the event. Post raw or template-framed. Reels are edited after the event (Day 1 recap can slip to Nov 15 morning).
5. **File organization:** shared drive folder `SA26_DAY1/` and `SA26_DAY2/` with subfolders per batch. Naming: `SA26_D1_0930_opening_01.jpg` (event_day_time_moment_number).
6. **Transfer:** photographer dumps to shared folder (Google Drive / WhatsApp) every 2 hours; poster pulls best 10 per batch.
7. **Consent:** printed consent form at registration (photo/video release). Verbal consent before every interview. Minors: require guardian signature; do not publish minors' faces without it. Respect anyone who opts out (wristband color system recommended).
8. **Hardware:** 1 phone per person + power banks, charger near posting station, backup SD/cloud sync.

## 4. RISKS / BLOCKERS & SOLUTIONS

| Risk | Likelihood | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Team burns out by week 4 | Medium | Lean calendar already applied; cut NICE-to-haves, keep education + challenge files |
| No official Mauritius event page yet | Medium | All local details are placeholders; publish global/official facts only until verified |
| Challenge statements release changes details | Low | Challenge Files before Oct 28 use official summaries only; update template if needed |
| Low registrations at mid-campaign | Medium | Activate stakeholder outreach, university clubs, comment-your-skill reels; honest checkpoint posts |
| Social team has no event-day bandwidth | Medium | Pre-schedule everything; use batch system; borrow 1 shooter |
| Partnership promises fall through | Medium | Never announce until signed; use generic stakeholder thanks |
| Negative comments / misinformation | Low | Reply calmly with official FAQ links; never delete legitimate questions |
| Photos of minors without consent | Low | Guardian consent + wristband system + no minor close-ups |
| Analytics unavailable on new accounts | Low | Use manual counts (link clicks via bit.ly, comments) |

## 5. POST-EVENT SEQUENCE (Phase 2 — after Nov 15)

Immediate (Nov 15–16): thank-you post, participant reaction reposts, LinkedIn recap.
Week 1 after event (Nov 16–22):
1. Event recap carousel (numbers + moments, real numbers only).
2. Best-moments Reel.
3. Group photo + moment photo dumps.
4. Volunteer appreciation post.
5. Organizer appreciation post.
6. Partner appreciation posts (LinkedIn-first).
7. Team/project spotlights #1–2 (FROM IDEA TO IMPACT) — with team consent.
8. "What happened at Space Apps Mauritius?" explainer for newcomers.
Weeks 2–4: participant testimonials, remaining project spotlights, lesson/impact post.
Official milestones to cover when confirmed: December 2026 Global Nominees/Finalists/Honorable Mentions; January 2027 Global Winners. Local winners only if local judging exists [VERIFY].

## 6. UGC SYSTEM

- **What participants post:** intro ("Hi, I'm X, I bring Y, eyeing challenge Z"), "my challenge pick", event-day progress, post-event "what I built in 48h".
- **Tag:** account handle + official `#SpaceApps` + proposed local tag `#SpaceAppsMauritius` (PROPOSED — verify no conflict before use).
- **Repost:** organizers repost with permission, credit the creator, save to Community highlight.
- **Incentive:** best intro/coverage featured on feed + a story shoutout; no prize promised unless local prize confirmed [VERIFY].
