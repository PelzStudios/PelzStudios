## C. OBSERVED CHAPTER PRACTICES (patterns, not policy)

Observed across the public feeds of the global NASA Space Apps accounts and local chapter accounts (general patterns, 2024–2025 seasons; no single chapter cited, no captions copied):

- Successful chapters announce early, then spend most of the campaign EDUCATING (what, who, how) rather than advertising.
- Challenge explainers work best as templated carousels + short reels, not copied text from official pages.
- Live events are covered heavily in Stories (volume, immediacy) and selectively in feed (quality).
- Team-formation posts that ask a question ("comment your skill") generate the most comments.
- LinkedIn posts from organizers themselves outperform anonymous page posts with professionals.
- Post-event content (recaps, project stories, thank-yous) drives the most saves/follows retention.

These are observations, not official guidance.
